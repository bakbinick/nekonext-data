Frequently asked questions

What is NekoNext?

NekoNext is a free Chrome extension for watching series and anime. It lets you watch together with friends in sync (with chat and an optional voice room), plays the next episode automatically, skips openings and blocks pop-up ads on video sites.

Which sites does NekoNext work on?

Auto next episode and watch together work on almost any video or anime streaming site: you turn NekoNext on for the site from the extension menu once. On Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, HBO Max and Crunchyroll it works right after install and presses the sites' own "Skip Intro" and "Next Episode" buttons. Music rooms work on YouTube, YouTube Music and SoundCloud.

How do I watch anime together with friends?

Open the video, click the NekoNext icon, go to the Room tab and press "Open a room". Send the invite link or the 6-letter room code to your friends. Once they join, play, pause, seeking and the next episode happen for everyone at the same time, and you can chat, send stickers and talk in the voice room (up to 6 people).

Is NekoNext a Teleparty alternative?

Yes, if you want to watch together. Many watch-party extensions only support a list of big streaming services. NekoNext also works on most other video and anime sites, and in the same extension it plays the next episode automatically, skips openings and blocks pop-ups. The watch-together features are free.

Is NekoNext free?

Yes. All current features are free and stay free, with no ads and no account. An optional paid NekoNext Pro adds extras such as animated room themes and a Neko sticker pack; you can try an animated theme for 60 seconds in any room.

Do my friends need to install it too?

Yes, everyone in a room needs NekoNext. When a friend without it opens your invite link, they see a short page that helps them install it and join the room.

How does auto next episode work?

When an episode ends, NekoNext shows a short countdown and opens the next episode. It finds the next episode from the site's episode list, from the page address, or from a "Next" button you show it once. You can start it a few seconds before the ending and stop after a set number of episodes.

Can it skip anime openings?

Yes. Press O when the opening starts and O again when it ends: NekoNext remembers it and skips the opening in every episode of that series. You can also set one opening length for all series, or press S to jump ahead.

Which browsers are supported? Does it work on phones?

NekoNext is made for Google Chrome on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS. Most Chromium-based browsers, such as Microsoft Edge and Brave, can also install extensions from the Chrome Web Store. Mobile browsers do not support extensions, so it does not work on phones.

Is it private?

NekoNext has no account, no tracking and no analytics. Your settings and watch history stay in your browser. Room features connect to our server only while you are in a room, and chat is not stored. See the privacy policy for details.
