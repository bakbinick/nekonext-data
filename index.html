Next episode, automatically.

NekoNext is a free browser extension for series and anime. It plays the next episode for you, skips openings and keeps pop-up ads out of the way.

Get in touchComing soon to the Chrome Web Store

Auto next episode

A short countdown when an episode ends, then the next one starts. Works on almost any streaming site.

Skip openings

Mark a series' opening once with the O key. NekoNext skips it in every episode after that.

Continue watching

Pick up at the exact second you left off, with a weekly summary of what you watched.

Pop-up protection

Blocks pop-up windows and ad tabs and undoes redirects to ad sites.

Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, HBO Max

Presses the sites' own "Skip Intro" and "Next Episode" buttons for you.

Private and free

No account, no tracking. Your settings stay in your browser. 12 languages.
